SpaceKit is installed

  1. Open SpaceKit from your Applications folder.
  2. For complete results, give it Full Disk Access: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Full Disk Access. Without it, macOS hides some apps' data, and SpaceKit shows that space as Hidden.
  3. Set up your first cleanup job in Automation.

In Terminal, spacekit doctor checks permissions, config, rules and the background agent.
