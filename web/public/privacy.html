The SpaceKit app

SpaceKit scans and cleans your disk locally. It has no accounts, no analytics and no telemetry, and it doesn’t send information about you, your Mac or your files to us or anyone else. Its settings, history and cleanup journal stay in files on your Mac.

Visiting this website

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or third-party scripts. Like any web server, ours briefly logs technical request data (IP address, time, page requested, browser type) to keep the site running and secure. These logs are kept for at most 30 days.

Buying SpaceKit
