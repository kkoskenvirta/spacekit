          Could an automatic job delete something I need?
          
    Automatic jobs only remove data a rule recognises as regenerable, such as build output and package caches, unless you explicitly opt a job in to more. They never touch your home or personal folders, system folders, credentials or git repositories, and they stay under a per-run byte budget. By default everything goes to the Trash, and every removal is journaled.

          Which Macs does it run on?
          
    Any Mac running macOS 15 Sequoia or later, with Apple silicon or Intel.
    
        
    
        
    
          Does it need Full Disk Access?
          
    It works without it, but macOS then hides some apps’ data (Mail, Messages and so on) and SpaceKit reports that space as “Hidden”. For the complete picture, grant Full Disk Access in System Settings → Privacy & Security.
    
        
    
        
    
          Does the background agent slow my Mac down?
          
    No. It’s a small per-user launchd job that wakes up periodically (hourly by default), runs any jobs that are due and goes back to sleep. If your Mac was asleep at the scheduled time, the job runs at the next check.
    
        
    
        
    
          Can I use it from the terminal?
          
    Yes. The spacekit CLI and the full-screen terminal UI (spacekit tui) are included, and they share one YAML config with the app. Most commands take --json for scripting.
