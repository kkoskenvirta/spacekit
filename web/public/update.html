Save your license key

This is your SpaceKit license. It gets you free updates and lets you download SpaceKit again any time. Stripe’s receipt doesn’t include it, so save it now: copy it to your password manager or notes, or download it as a file.

Updating: paste the key in SpaceKit → Settings → Updates, or click Link to SpaceKit below. Lost SpaceKit? Open your download link, or paste the key at spacekit.studio/update. Keep it private: anyone with it can download your copy.

  1. Quit SpaceKit if it’s running.
  2. Open the downloaded SpaceKit installer and follow the steps. It replaces the old version.
  3. Open SpaceKit again. Your settings, cleanup jobs, history and journal are kept. Click Link to SpaceKit to store your license key in the app, and from now on SpaceKit installs updates itself.
