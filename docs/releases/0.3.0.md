**SpaceKit 0.3.0**

- **One-click updates.** SpaceKit now downloads, verifies and installs updates itself, then relaunches. No more dragging disk images around.
- Turn on *Download and install updates automatically* in Settings → Updates to get new versions without lifting a finger.
- Updates are free and need your purchase linked once: click *Link to SpaceKit* on your download page, or paste your purchase key in Settings → Updates.
