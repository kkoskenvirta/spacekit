**SpaceKit 0.2.0**

- **Updates inside the app.** SpaceKit now checks for new versions once a day and shows a banner when one is out. Turn it off in Settings → Updates.
- **Link your purchase.** Click *Link to SpaceKit* on your download page, or paste your purchase key in Settings → Updates, and Download gets you the newest version straight away. Updates are free.
- *Check for Updates…* in the SpaceKit menu.
